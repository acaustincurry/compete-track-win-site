How it works

Three steps to compete

1

Download and connect

Grab it from the App Store, sign in with Apple or Google, and connect Apple Health. That's the whole setup. No wearable? No problem — Daily Steps scores straight from your phone.

2

Join or start a challenge

Have an invite code? Joining friends' challenges is always free — no limits. Or start your own: a group challenge, or a one-tap head-to-head Call Out. Add an optional Challenge Note — bragging rights, or who picks the next workout.

3

Climb the leaderboard

Just train like you already do. Verified workouts sync from Apple Health and score automatically — 1 point for every active minute your heart rate backs up — and the live leaderboard settles it. Keep your streak alive, and check your readiness to know when to push and when to ease off.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need a wearable?

No. If your workouts land in Apple Health — from a watch or any connected device — they sync automatically. And if you don't have a wearable, you can compete on Daily Steps straight from your phone.

Is it free?

Yes. The full loop — Call Outs, challenges, leaderboards, streaks, and the team feed — is free, and joining a friend's challenge is always free, with no limit; the free tier only limits how many open challenges you can create (a sent Call Out counts until it's accepted or expires). Every new account starts with a 7-day Pro trial, and Pro ($7.99/mo or $39.99/yr) adds deeper history and coaching.

How do you stop cheating?

Every workout is recorded by a device — your watch, a connected fitness app, or your phone's step count — and scored on our servers. No manual entries, no typed-in numbers. Earned, not typed.
