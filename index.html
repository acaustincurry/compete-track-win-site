How it works

Three steps to compete

1

Work out — we verify it

Your workouts sync automatically from Apple Watch, Garmin, Whoop, Coros, or Fitbit via Apple Health. Real, verified effort — earn 1 point per active minute.

2

Bring your team

Follow your teammates' workouts in a shared feed, cheer each other on, and start a group challenge or a one-tap head-to-head Call Out.

3

Stay accountable

Climb a live leaderboard, keep your streak alive, and check your readiness so you train smart. Add an optional friendly stake to commit — settled directly between friends.

The promise

We never touch the money.

Compete. Track. Win. is a fitness tracking and social app. Any friendly stakes are arranged and settled directly between participants — through whatever they already use, like Venmo, Cash App, or cash. The app never collects, holds, or transfers funds. We just keep an honest, verified score.
