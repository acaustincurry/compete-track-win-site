How it works

Three steps to compete

1

Start a challenge — or call out a friend

Pick the activity, the length, and optional friendly stakes. Share a link, or call out a rival one-on-one. They're in the moment they accept.

2

Move. We verify.

Workouts sync automatically from Apple Watch and Apple Health — real, verified effort. Handicaps keep it fair, so beginners and pros both have a shot.

3

Climb the standing

Watch a live leaderboard shift as your crew logs work. When it ends, you settle up directly with each other. The app never holds a cent.

The promise

We never touch the money.

Compete. Track. Win. is a fitness tracking and social app. Any friendly stakes are arranged and settled directly between participants — through whatever they already use, like Venmo, Cash App, or cash. The app never collects, holds, or transfers funds. We just keep an honest, verified score.
