FAQ

Good questions

Do I need a wearable?

No. If your workouts land in Apple Health — from a watch or any connected device — they sync automatically. And if you don't have a wearable, you can compete on Daily Steps straight from your phone.

Is it free?

Yes. The full loop — Call Outs, challenges, leaderboards, streaks, and the team feed — is free. Pro ($7.99/mo, or $39.99/yr with a 7-day trial) adds deeper history and coaching.

How do you stop cheating?

Every workout is recorded by your wearable and scored on our servers — no manual entries, no typed-in numbers. Earned, not typed.
